# Priority Data Sources — Phase 0

## 1. Official Textbooks (Highest Priority)

Focus order:

### Mathematics
- Grade 9 Mathematics
- Grade 10 Mathematics (1 & 2 if split)
- Grade 11 Mathematics
- Grade 12 Mathematics (and/or Calculus / Discrete if applicable)

### Physics
- Grade 10, 11, 12 Physics

### Chemistry
- Grade 10, 11, 12 Chemistry

### Biology
- Grade 10, 11, 12 Biology

### Other high-value
- Persian Literature (selected analytical chapters)
- Arabic (grammar + translation sections useful for reasoning)

> Source preference: Official Roshd / Ministry of Education PDFs when available.

---

## 2. Exam Questions

### Konkur (National University Entrance Exam)
- Recent years (at least last 8–10 years)
- Prefer versions with detailed solutions / explanations

### Tizhooshan
- Entrance exams for gifted schools

### Olympiad
- Stage 1 and Stage 2 problems
- Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Informatics

---

## 3. Data Quality Targets

For each source we want:
- Clean text (minimal OCR noise)
- Proper pedagogical structure (definition → example → exercise)
- Metadata: subject, grade, topic, difficulty, source type

See `data/SCHEMA.md` for the target SFT format.
