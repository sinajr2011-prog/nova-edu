# Step-by-step Visual Solutions

## Goal
Turn any solution into a clear sequence of visual steps (images or short animations).

## Flow
1. Problem Solver produces a structured step-by-step text solution
2. Visual Explainer converts each major step into a diagram or short animation frame
3. The result can be shown as:
   - A sequence of images
   - A short video that moves through the steps

## Why it matters
Many students understand much better when they *see* the solution unfold rather than only reading it.
